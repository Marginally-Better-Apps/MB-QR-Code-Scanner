# Store screenshot photos

Real photos of QR codes in the wild, used for App Store screenshots by `scripts/capture-store-screenshots.sh`. Both are CC0 (public domain) on Wikimedia Commons, so no attribution is required. They were cropped and resized, and their metadata was removed.

| File | Used for | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `camera-wifi-card.jpg` | Camera screenshot (shown through the camera fixture, decoded live) | [Din Tai Fung Wi-Fi card, Taipei](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:TW_%E5%8F%B0%E7%81%A3_Taiwan_%E5%8F%B0%E5%8C%97_Taipei_Din_Tai_Fung_QR_code_March_2024_R12S_01.jpg), CC0. Cropped to the card, without the date stamp. |
| `campground-sign.jpg` | Photo import screenshot (added to the Simulator Photos library) | [Land of Memories Campground sign](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Land_of_Memories_Campground_map_and_information_sign.jpg) by Pingnova, CC0. |

The Wi-Fi card decodes to `WIFI:S:DTFWIFI;…`, and the campground sign decodes to `https://qrco.de/bdAAOz`.
