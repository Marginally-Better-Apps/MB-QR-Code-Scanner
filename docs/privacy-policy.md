# QR Scanner privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

QR Scanner processes camera frames on your device to read codes. It does not upload or save camera images. Scan History stays on your device and may be included in your normal device backup. You can delete individual scans or clear History in the app. Authentication secrets, Wi-Fi passwords, and raw boarding passes are not saved in History.

If you choose **Look Up Product**, the app sends the barcode number to the Open Food Facts service. That request also exposes the network information normally sent with an internet request, such as your IP address. Open Food Facts provides the product data and handles the request under its [privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy). The app does not send images or your scan History to that service.

Opening a link or using a share action sends information to the destination you choose. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

QR Scanner does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. We do not run a server that receives your scans. To ask about this policy or the app, email [help@marginally-better.app](mailto:help@marginally-better.app).
